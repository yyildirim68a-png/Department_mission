# Department_mission

## William Newman Department of Real Estate — Research Identity and Strategic Direction

**Working draft for faculty discussion | November 23, 2026**  
**Baruch College, Zicklin School of Business, City University of New York**

> **The Financial Economics of Real Estate**  
> **Capital, Intermediation, and Asset Valuation**  
> *From Property Transactions to Capital Markets*

This repository organizes a proposed, evidence-based research identity for the William Newman Department of Real Estate. It maps six research faculty members' published and ongoing work to common research questions, identifies current capabilities and areas for growth, and proposes a faculty-hiring direction for the next decade.

**This is a strategic discussion draft, not an officially adopted department mission.**

### Research positioning

Our faculty study how **capital, credit, financial institutions, investors, contracts, and market frictions** shape the **financing, trading, pricing, and allocation** of real estate assets. Their work connects property-level markets with mortgage and securities markets and contributes to broader questions in asset pricing, financial intermediation, and investment.

### Scope

Research faculty covered here: Yildiray Yildirim, Wei Li, Maggie Hu, Sophia Gilbukh, Joshua Coven, and Heejin Yoon. Lecturer and practitioner teaching roles are not used to measure the scholarly research identity. Individual job candidates and confidential search deliberations are **not** included.

The source CVs were provided for this analysis but are deliberately **not uploaded** here. Faculty CVs can contain personal contact details and should be shared separately only with the appropriate permissions.
