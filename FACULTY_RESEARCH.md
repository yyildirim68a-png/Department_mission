# Research faculty: evidence and contribution to the shared identity

**Scope:** Six research faculty members only.  
**Sources:** Faculty CVs supplied in November 2026 and the publication/working-paper listings on Yildiray Yildirim's website. See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

The selections below are representative evidence, not complete publication lists. **Published** means the source indicates a published or accepted peer-reviewed article. **R&R**, **submitted**, and **working paper** are separate categories and must not be confused with published work.

## Yildiray Yildirim

**Central contribution:** Financial asset pricing, structured credit, commercial real estate debt, leases, and valuation under risk.

- **Published:** *Risk Retention Rules and the Issuance of Commercial Mortgage Backed Securities*, *Journal of Real Estate Finance and Economics* (2024).
- **Published:** *Capital Structure and the Substitutability versus Complementary Nature of Leases and Debt*, *Review of Finance* (2019).
- **Published:** *Liquidity Constraints, Consumption, and Debt Repayment: Evidence from Macroprudential Policy in Turkey*, *Review of Financial Studies* (2023).
- **Published:** *The Impact of Tenant Diversification on Commercial Mortgage Spread and Default Rates*, *Journal of Real Estate Finance and Economics* (2018).
- **Published:** *The Term Structure of Lease Rates with Endogenous Default Triggers and Tenant Capital Structure: Theory and Evidence*, *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis* (2011).
- **Working papers:** *Valuing Illiquid Residential Real Estate with Embedded Default Risk: Application to Rent-to-Own Contracts*; projects on CMBS, climate risk, and affordable-housing municipal bonds.

**Identity connection:** Links illiquid property cash flows and financial contracts to mortgage securities, default risk, credit pricing, and no-arbitrage valuation.

## Wei Li

**Central contribution:** Asset pricing and market microstructure in mortgage securities and OTC markets.

- **Published:** *Asset Pricing with Cohort-Based Trading in MBS Markets*, *Journal of Finance* (2022).
- **Published:** *TBA Trading and Security Issuance in the Agency MBS Market*, *Real Estate Economics* (2025).
- **R&R according to March 2026 CV:** *Dealer Expertise and Market Concentration in OTC Trading*, *Journal of Economic Theory*.
- **In progress:** *Agency and Private-label Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities* (with Yildirim and Song), as listed in the supplied CV.

**Identity connection:** Shows how dealer incentives, trading institutions, and market fragmentation influence securities backed by real estate.

## Maggie Hu

**Central contribution:** Property rights, real estate contracts, investment behavior, financial institutions, and housing market dynamics.

- **Published:** *Valuing Long-Term Property Rights with Anticipated Political Regime Shifts*, *American Economic Review* (2024).
- **Published:** *Tobin Tax Policy, Housing Speculation, and Property Market Dynamics*, *Management Science* (2026).
- **Published:** *Corporate Relocation and Housing Market Spillovers*, *Management Science* (2025).
- **Published:** *Information Asymmetry and Organizational Structure: Evidence from REITs*, *Journal of Real Estate Finance and Economics* (2017).
- **Published:** *Commercial Real Estate Rental Index: A Dynamic Panel Data Model Estimation*, *Real Estate Economics* (2016).
- **R&R according to Fall 2026 CV:** *Haste or Waste? The Role of Presale in Residential Housing*, *American Economic Journal: Microeconomics*.

**Identity connection:** Connects property-rights valuation, investment contracts, housing search, and price dynamics with finance and economic incentives. Not all of her corporate-finance publications are real estate papers.

## Sophia Gilbukh

**Central contribution:** Brokers, housing transaction intermediation, search frictions, information, and financing discrimination.

- **Published:** *Heterogeneous Real Estate Agents and the Housing Cycle*, *Review of Financial Studies* (2024).
- **Published:** *The Price to Rent Ratio: A Macroprudential Application*, *Real Estate Economics* (2022/2023 in CV citation).
- **R&R according to Fall 2026 CV:** *More Experienced Intermediaries — More Principal-Agent Problems*, *Journal of Urban Economics*.
- **Submitted according to CV:** *New Listings Alert: Alternative Approach to Modeling Housing Search*, *Review of Financial Studies*.
- **Submitted according to CV:** *Unintended Consequences of FHA/VA Government Programs*, *Journal of Finance*.

**Identity connection:** Shows how search, intermediary incentives, and access to financing affect the liquidity, price, and allocation of directly traded real estate.

## Joshua Coven

**Central contribution:** Institutional investors, housing ownership and access, and financially constrained allocation.

- **Published:** *Urban flight seeded the COVID-19 pandemic across the United States*, *Journal of Urban Economics* (2023), with coauthors.
- **R&R according to June 2026 CV:** *The Impact of Institutional Investors on Homeownership and Neighborhood Access*, *Review of Financial Studies*.
- **R&R according to June 2026 CV:** *Property Taxes and Housing Allocation Under Financial Constraints*, *American Economic Review*, with coauthors.

**Identity connection:** Explains how institutional investors and financial constraints change property ownership, access, and allocation. R&Rs are important pipeline evidence, not accepted publications.

## Heejin Yoon

**Central contribution:** Mortgage origination, refinancing and nonbank lender frictions, and commercial landlord financial distress.

- **Job market paper (March 2026 CV):** *Barriers to Reentry: Initial Borrowing Frictions, Refinancing, and Wealth Redistribution*.
- **Working paper:** *Nonbank Growth and Local Housing Booms*.
- **R&R according to March 2026 CV:** *Retail Apocalypse and the Incidence of Loss: Landlord Financial Distress and Tenant Closures*, *Real Estate Economics*.
- **Published:** *Does Venture Capital Investment Enhance Corporate Innovation? Evidence from Korea*, *Journal of Business Finance & Accounting* (2022), with coauthors. This is a corporate-finance publication and should not be reclassified as a real-estate paper.

**Identity connection:** Connects borrowers' financing frictions to subsequent refinancing and wealth outcomes, while the landlord distress project bridges credit and commercial property outcomes.

## Shared intellectual proposition

The six scholars do **not** have one identical subject. Their evidence supports a shared research question: **How do financing structures, intermediaries, market frictions, and investment decisions determine the trading, pricing, and allocation of real estate assets?**

This is a proposed organizing framework rather than a claim that every project or every journal publication fits it.
