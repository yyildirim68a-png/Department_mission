## Evidence standards

1. Separate **peer-reviewed published/accepted papers** from **revise-and-resubmit**, **submitted**, and **working-paper/in-progress** status.
2. Do not infer that a journal submission or R&R will be published.
3. A title and journal in a CV support the existence of that listed research item; they do not alone establish independent authorship contributions, causal validity, or future research quality.
4. Do not infer the department's official approval of this document. All mission and hiring language is proposed until approved.
5. Preserve each faculty member's distinctive agenda; the shared framework is a synthesis, not a replacement for research specifics.
6. Recheck CVs and the public publication pages before using these summaries externally, because status and publications change over time.
